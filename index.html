📅 我的行事曆：只看跟你有關的會議

  1. 點上方的「我的行事曆」頁籤。
  2. 第一次使用時選你的名字就好，這台瀏覽器會記住，之後不用再選。
  3. 你主辦或受邀參加的會議都會集中在這裡。可以切換月／週／日，也可以用「只看我的／全部」切換要不要看其他人的會議。
  4. 想臨時訂會議室，直接點空白時段的「＋ 新增」就能預約。

🔔 桌面通知：會議有變動，馬上知道

  1. 點名字旁邊的鈴鐺，瀏覽器問你要不要允許通知時，選「允許」。
  2. 開啟後會馬上跳一則測試通知，看到就代表設定成功。
  3. 之後遇到這些情況會通知你：
    • 有新會議把你加進去
    • 你的會議改了時間或會議室
    • 你的會議被取消，或你被移出與會名單
    • 會議開始前 10 分鐘

小提醒

  • 通知只在這個網頁開著的時候才收得到。可以放在背景分頁，但不要關掉。
  • 你自己做的新增、修改或取消，不會再通知你自己。

按了允許卻沒看到測試通知？

  • Windows：到「設定 → 系統 → 通知」，確認通知有開、你用的瀏覽器也有允許，並關掉「勿擾」。
  • Mac：第一次通知時，系統可能會另外問一次，請選「允許」。如果之前按過拒絕，到「系統設定 → 通知」找到你用的瀏覽器（例如 Google Chrome、Safari），把「允許通知」打開，並關掉「專注模式」。

📅 My Calendar: just the meetings that involve you

  1. Click the My Calendar tab at the top.
  2. The first time, just pick your name. This browser will remember it, so you only do this once.
  3. Every meeting you organize or attend shows up here. Switch between Month / Week / Day, and use Mine / All to show or hide other people's meetings.
  4. Need a room quickly? Click + Add on any empty time slot to book it.

🔔 Desktop notifications: know right away when a meeting changes

  1. Click the bell next to your name, then choose Allow when your browser asks.
  2. A test notification pops up right away. If you see it, you're all set.
  3. From then on, you'll be notified when:
    • You're added to a new meeting
    • One of your meetings changes time or room
    • One of your meetings is cancelled, or you're removed from it
    • A meeting starts in 10 minutes

Tips

  • Notifications only arrive while this page is open. A background tab is fine, just don't close it.
  • You won't be notified about bookings you add, change, or cancel yourself.

Allowed it but didn't see the test notification?

  • Windows: Go to Settings → System → Notifications. Make sure notifications are on, your browser is allowed, and Do not disturb is off.
  • Mac: macOS may ask again the first time a notification appears. Choose Allow. If you declined before, go to System Settings → Notifications, find your browser (e.g. Google Chrome or Safari), turn on Allow notifications, and turn off Focus.
